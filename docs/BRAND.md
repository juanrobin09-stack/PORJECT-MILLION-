# Sona — Brand Book

## Name

**Sona** — from *sonus*: sound, signal. What customers say, made audible to
the company. Four letters, pronounceable everywhere, verb-able ("route it
through Sona"). Evolved from v1's "Resona" — the platform kept the signal,
dropped the echo.

Domain direction: `sona.dev` (API-first positioning), `getsona.com` redirect.

## One-liner

> **Everything your customers say. One schema. Acted on.**

## Positioning statement

For companies whose customer feedback is scattered across reviews, tickets,
NPS, and chats, Sona is the customer signal layer: one canonical schema with
AI enrichment, automations, plain-language answers, and industry benchmarks —
unlike experience-management suites that sell five dashboards and no shared
truth.

## Audience-tuned pitches

- **Developer:** "Segment for customer feedback. POST anything your customers
  say; get back canonical, enriched, automatable signals — and an `/ask`
  endpoint that answers questions about them."
- **Operator (the wedge):** "Every review answered in your voice within the
  hour — and you'll know first if something dangerous appears."
- **Executive:** "One number you can't get anywhere else: how your customer
  complaints compare to your industry's."
- **Investor:** "The system of record for customer feedback, with a taxonomy
  network effect: every tenant makes the benchmark dataset more valuable, and
  nobody can copy an installed base speaking one schema."

## Voice & tone

- **Specific over slick.** Never "unlock insights"; always "9 of 31 detractor
  verbatims mention billing."
- **Calm authority.** Infrastructure doesn't shout. The night-watchman tone of
  v1 survives in the risk-alerting surface.
- **Engineer-grade honesty.** We say "k-anonymity threshold not yet met in
  your industry" rather than faking density.

## Launch copy blocks

**Directory short description (80 chars):**
> One API for everything your customers say — enriched, automated, benchmarked.

**Elevator (25 words):**
> Sona turns every review, ticket, and NPS verbatim into one canonical,
> AI-enriched stream you can query, automate, and benchmark against your
> industry.

**Objection one-liners:**
- *"We have a reputation tool."* — For one channel, with no schema under it.
  What answers "what do detractors say that promoters don't"?
- *"We could build this."* — The pipeline, maybe. The cross-industry benchmark
  dataset requires everyone else's tenants. That's the part you can't build.
- *"Is our data training your benchmarks?"* — Aggregates only, no tenant IDs,
  published solely above the k-anonymity threshold. Your raw signals never
  leave your tenant.
