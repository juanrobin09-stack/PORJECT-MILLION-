# Resona — Brand Book

## Name

**Resona** — from *resonance*: the way a brand's voice carries, and the way a
single review echoes across thousands of future customers. Short, pronounceable
in every target market, verb-able ("just Resona it").

Domain direction: `resona.app` (primary), `getresona.com` (redirect).

## One-liner

> **Every review answered. In your voice. Within the hour.**

## Positioning statement

For multi-location consumer businesses drowning in reviews, Resona is the AI
reputation engine that answers every review in the brand's own voice and
catches dangerous ones before they spread — unlike legacy reputation suites
that sell dashboards and templates at triple the price.

## Voice & tone

Resona's own voice mirrors what it writes for customers — proof of product:

- **Specific over slick.** Never "boost your online presence"; always "answer
  the 2-star from Tuesday before lunch."
- **Calm authority.** We deal in reputational risk; we never use fear-mongering
  punctuation. The night-watchman doesn't shout.
- **Human warmth, zero corporate filler.** "We apologize for any inconvenience"
  is banned in our marketing for the same reason customers ban it in their
  brand-voice profiles.

## Launch copy blocks

**App-store / directory short description (80 chars):**
> AI that answers every customer review in your voice — and flags the risky ones.

**Elevator (25 words):**
> Resona reads every review the moment it lands, drafts a reply your best
> manager would sign, and alerts you when one could hurt you.

**Cold-email opener (sales-assisted phase):**
> Subject: 14 unanswered reviews at {Business}
>
> {FirstName} — last week {Business} got 14 reviews. Your competitors answered
> theirs; yours are still sitting there, including a 1-star about {topic}.
> Here's the reply we'd have posted within the hour: {AI draft}. Want the other
> 13?

**Objection one-liners:**
- *"AI will sound robotic."* — It sounds like you on your best day; you
  approve every word until you decide not to.
- *"We already respond."* — To all of them? Within an hour? In the reviewer's
  language? At 11pm on Saturday?
- *"What if it says something wrong?"* — Risky reviews are hard-blocked from
  auto-publish in code, not in a prompt — they wait for you, and you get paged.
